# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be civil. Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith and is
probably busy.

## What that rules out

Personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, sustained disruption of
threads, and publishing others' private information.

## What it does not rule out

Disagreement. Saying an approach is wrong, that a patch should not land, or that
a decision was a mistake is normal engineering. Directness is not rudeness.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Reporting

Email conduct@ansa.so. Reports go to the maintainers and stay private.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, when this
document is not being followed.

This is adapted loosely from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
trimmed to what a project this size can actually enforce.
