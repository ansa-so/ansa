Here's how to cut your Shopify support tickets by 80% using AI — and which tickets to target first.

## The Anatomy of a Shopify Support Queue

Before automating anything, you need to understand what's actually filling your inbox. Most Shopify stores see a remarkably similar breakdown:

- **Where is my order? (WISMO)** — 30-50% of all tickets. Customers want tracking updates, delivery estimates, and reassurance their package isn't lost.
- **Returns and exchanges** — 15-25%. Return eligibility, how to initiate a return, refund timelines, exchange availability.
- **Product questions** — 10-20%. Sizing, compatibility, materials, "will this work with X?"
- **Order modifications** — 5-10%. Cancel my order, change shipping address, add an item.
- **Discount and promo issues** — 5-8%. Code not working, stacking rules, expired promotions.
- **Account and login issues** — 3-5%. Password resets, order history access, account creation.

<Callout variant="info">
That's 70-90% of tickets that follow predictable patterns with clear answers. These are the tickets AI handles well — not because they're simple, but because the information needed to resolve them already exists in your systems.
</Callout>

The remaining 10-30% — billing disputes, complex product issues, angry customers who need empathy — those stay with your human team. And they'll handle them better when they're not buried under 200 WISMO tickets a day.

## Which Tickets to Automate First

Not all automatable tickets are equal. Prioritize by volume × resolution complexity:

### Tier 1: WISMO (Automate Immediately)

WISMO tickets are the highest-volume, lowest-complexity category. An AI agent connected to your Shopify order data can:

- Pull real-time tracking status from Shopify, AfterShip, or your 3PL
- Provide estimated delivery dates
- Detect and proactively flag delayed shipments
- Explain what "in transit" or "out for delivery" actually means

A single WISMO integration typically eliminates 30-40% of your entire ticket volume overnight.

### Tier 2: Product Questions (Automate Next)

Product questions seem harder to automate, but they're not — if your product pages are well-documented. An AI agent that indexes your product catalog, FAQ pages, and sizing guides can answer:

- "Does this come in size XL?"
- "Is this compatible with iPhone 15?"
- "What's the material?"
- "How does this compare to [other product]?"

The key is connecting the AI to your actual product data, not writing scripted responses for every possible question.

### Tier 3: Returns and Exchanges

Returns require both knowledge (your return policy) and action (initiating the return). This is where most basic chatbots fail — they can recite the policy but can't actually process the return.

An AI agent with Shopify API access can:

- Check if the order is within the return window
- Verify the item is eligible for return
- Generate a return shipping label
- Process the exchange or refund
- Send confirmation emails

This moves returns from a 3-email, 24-hour process to a 2-minute conversation.

### Tier 4: Order Modifications

Changing a shipping address or canceling an order requires direct Shopify API calls. An AI agent that can modify orders mid-conversation handles these instantly — no human needed unless the order has already shipped.
