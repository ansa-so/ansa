Gorgias is one of the most popular helpdesks in the Shopify ecosystem — and for good reason. It was purpose-built for ecommerce, integrates deeply with Shopify, and has a great reputation among DTC brands.

But there's a growing number of small Shopify stores that loved Gorgias at first and quietly left. The reason is almost always the same: pricing.

## Why Shopify Stores Love Gorgias (At First)

Credit where it's due — Gorgias nails the Shopify helpdesk experience:

- **Deep Shopify sidebar** — Order data, customer history, and refund actions right inside the ticket view. No tab-switching.
- **Macros and rules** — Automate repetitive tasks like tagging, assigning, and auto-responding based on order status.
- **Multi-channel inbox** — Email, chat, social, SMS — all in one place.
- **Ecommerce DNA** — Unlike generic helpdesks (Zendesk, Freshdesk), Gorgias was built specifically for online stores. It understands order lifecycles.

For a store doing 50-200 tickets/month, Gorgias works great. The Starter plan is affordable, the Shopify integration is best-in-class, and the learning curve is gentle.

The problems start when you grow.

## The Problem: Gorgias Pricing Gets Painful Fast

Gorgias prices by ticket volume. Every billable ticket — whether it's a simple "where's my order?" or a 10-message exchange about a damaged product — counts against your tier. Go over your limit and you pay overage fees.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

| Plan | Monthly Tickets | Price/Month | Overage Rate |
|------|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Starter | 50 | $10 | $0.40/ticket |
| Basic | 300 | $60 | $0.40/ticket |
| Pro | 2,000 | $360 | $0.36/ticket |
| Advanced | 5,000 | $900 | $0.36/ticket |

<Callout variant="warning">
The overage math is brutal during peak season. A store on the Basic plan (300 tickets) that gets 800 tickets during Black Friday pays $60 + (500 × $0.40) = **$260** for that month — 4x the base price. And that's before the AI add-on.
</Callout>

Gorgias also charges separately for their AI features. Their Automate add-on — which handles automated responses — costs an additional $0.50 per automated interaction on top of your plan. So you're paying twice: once for the ticket tier, again for the AI.

For a small store doing 500 tickets/month, the real monthly bill looks more like:

- Basic plan: $60
- Overage (200 tickets × $0.40): $80
- AI Automate (350 auto-resolved × $0.50): $175
- **Total: $315/month**

And it only goes up from there. A store hitting 1,000 tickets/month can easily spend $500-700/month on Gorgias — which is a lot for a business doing $30K-$100K/month in revenue.

## What Small Stores Actually Need From a Support Tool

After talking to hundreds of Shopify store owners who switched away from Gorgias, the requirements are remarkably consistent:

1. **Flat, predictable pricing** — No per-ticket billing. No overage anxiety. A number you can budget for.
2. **Shopify-native integration** — Not just reading order data, but taking action: processing returns, canceling orders, updating shipping addresses.
3. **AI-first, not AI-bolted-on** — The AI should be the core product, not a paid add-on layered onto a traditional helpdesk.
4. **Setup in hours, not weeks** — Small teams don't have a dedicated support ops person. The tool needs to work out of the box.
5. **Smart human handoff** — When the AI can't help, it should route to a human with full context — not dump the customer into a cold queue.

## Making the Switch: What to Expect

Migrating from Gorgias to any alternative is simpler than you'd think. The main steps:

1. **Export your macros and saved replies** — Most can be imported into your new tool or used as knowledge base content for AI training.
2. **Connect Shopify** — Every alternative listed here has a Shopify integration. Setup takes 5-15 minutes.
3. **Add your knowledge base** — Upload your FAQ, shipping policy, return policy, and product docs. For AI-first tools like Ansa, this is what powers the automation.
4. **Run both tools in parallel for 1-2 weeks** — Route a percentage of traffic to the new tool and compare resolution quality and speed.
5. **Cut over** — Once you're confident in the new tool, remove the Gorgias widget and cancel your plan.

The whole process typically takes 1-2 weeks, and you don't lose any customer data since everything lives in Shopify.
