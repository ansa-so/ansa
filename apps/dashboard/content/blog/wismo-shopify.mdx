"Where is my order?" Four words that account for more support tickets than any other question in ecommerce. If you run a Shopify store, you already know: WISMO isn't just a ticket type — it's the ticket type.

Industry data consistently puts WISMO at 40-60% of all ecommerce support volume. For a Shopify store processing 3,000 orders/month, that's 600-1,200 WISMO tickets every month. Each one takes 3-5 minutes for a human agent to resolve — pull up the order, check tracking, copy the status, craft a response, hit send.

That's 30-100 hours of agent time per month spent copying and pasting tracking numbers.

## Why WISMO Volume Is So High

Customers don't contact support because they're impatient (well, sometimes). They contact support because the default Shopify experience has gaps:

**Shopify's built-in tracking is limited.** The order status page shows basic tracking info, but it doesn't proactively update customers when shipments are delayed, doesn't explain carrier status codes in plain language, and disappears once the customer closes the tab.

**Shipping confirmation emails get buried.** Your customer received the tracking email, scrolled past it, and now can't find it. Rather than searching their inbox, they message you.

**Carrier tracking pages are confusing.** "In transit to next facility" — what does that mean? When will it arrive? Is it lost? The carrier page doesn't answer the question the customer is actually asking: "When will I get my stuff?"

**Anxiety peaks at specific moments.** WISMO tickets don't arrive evenly. They spike 2-3 days after order placement, again when tracking shows no movement for 48+ hours, and again the day before expected delivery.

## How Stores Handle WISMO Today (And Why It Doesn't Scale)

### The Manual Approach

Most small-to-mid Shopify stores handle WISMO the same way:

1. Customer sends "where's my order?" via email, chat, or Instagram DM
2. Agent asks for order number or email
3. Agent looks up the order in Shopify admin
4. Agent checks tracking on the carrier's site
5. Agent writes a response summarizing the status
6. Customer replies with a follow-up question
7. Agent checks again and responds

Average resolution: 2-3 messages over 4-8 hours. Cost: $5-12 per ticket.

This works when you're doing 50 orders a month. At 5,000 orders, you need dedicated agents just for WISMO.

### The Order Tracking Page Approach

Some stores add dedicated tracking pages using apps like AfterShip, Tracktor, or ParcelPanel. This helps — customers can self-serve by entering their order number on a branded tracking page.

But it only reduces WISMO by 20-30%. Why?

- Customers don't always know the page exists
- The tracking page doesn't answer "is my order lost?" or "can I change the delivery address?"
- When tracking shows no updates for 48 hours, customers still contact support for reassurance
- The page can't proactively reach out when there's a problem

### The Macro/Template Approach

Higher-volume stores create canned responses for common WISMO scenarios:

- "Your order is in transit and expected to arrive by [date]"
- "Your order hasn't shipped yet — our warehouse processes orders within 2 business days"
- "Your tracking shows it was delivered to [address] on [date]"

This speeds up agent response time but still requires a human to select the right macro, fill in the details, and handle edge cases. And it completely fails when the customer's question doesn't fit a template.

An AI agent connected to your Shopify store data doesn't just respond to WISMO — it resolves it. Here's the difference:

**AI Agent (connected to Shopify + carrier API):**

One message. Ten seconds. No human involved.

But the real power isn't just speed — it's the follow-up handling:

- **"Is it lost?"** → AI checks if the tracking has stalled beyond normal transit time and either reassures the customer or escalates to investigate.
- **"Can I change the delivery address?"** → AI checks if the carrier supports address changes and initiates one if possible. If not, explains why.
- **"It says delivered but I don't have it."** → AI escalates to a human agent with full context, since this likely needs a reshipment or refund decision.

### What AI Needs to Handle WISMO

For an AI agent to fully resolve WISMO tickets, it needs:

1. **Order data** — Order number, items, shipping method, fulfillment status (from Shopify API)
2. **Tracking data** — Real-time carrier tracking status (from Shopify, AfterShip, or carrier APIs)
3. **Policy knowledge** — Your shipping timelines, what to do when packages are delayed, reshipment criteria
4. **Action capability** — Ability to resend tracking emails, update orders, and escalate when needed
