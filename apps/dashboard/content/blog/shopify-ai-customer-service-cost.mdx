Every Shopify store owner eventually asks the same question: is AI customer service actually cheaper than hiring another support agent? The answer is almost always yes — but the gap between "cheap" and "expensive" AI depends entirely on the pricing model you choose.

Here's the full cost breakdown, with real math for stores at different scales.

## The Real Cost of Manual Customer Support

Before comparing AI pricing, you need to know what you're paying now. Most Shopify store owners underestimate their true support costs because they only think about salary.

The fully-loaded cost of a support ticket includes:

- **Agent wages** — A support agent costs $35,000-$55,000/year in the US, or $15-25/hour. Outsourced agents (Philippines, Latin America) run $8-15/hour.
- **Overhead** — Software licenses (Zendesk, Gorgias, Intercom), training, management time, QA reviews. Add 30-50% on top of wages.
- **Idle time** — Agents aren't resolving tickets 100% of the time. Factor in breaks, meetings, training, and downtime between tickets.

When you account for everything, the industry average cost per support ticket lands between **$8 and $22** depending on location, complexity, and tooling.

<Callout variant="info">
A simple WISMO ("where is my order?") ticket costs the same as a complex billing dispute in manual support — because agent time is agent time. AI flips this: simple tickets cost pennies while complex ones stay with humans.
</Callout>

For a Shopify store handling 1,000 tickets/month with a blended cost of $12/ticket, that's **$12,000/month** in support costs. And it scales linearly — 2x the orders means roughly 2x the tickets means 2x the cost.

## How AI Pricing Models Work

AI customer service tools use three main pricing models, and the differences are dramatic at scale:

### Per-Ticket / Per-Resolution Pricing

Platforms like **Gorgias** and **Intercom** charge per automated resolution — typically $0.50-$1.00 per ticket the AI handles. Sounds cheap per ticket, but the bill grows with every conversation.

- Gorgias: $0.50/automated resolution (on top of base plan)
- Intercom: $0.99/AI resolution (Fin AI Agent)

The trap: as your AI gets better and resolves more tickets, your bill goes up. You're penalized for success.

### Per-Seat / Per-Agent Pricing

**Zendesk** charges per agent seat — $55-115/agent/month depending on plan tier, plus $1.00/automated resolution for their AI add-on. **Freshdesk** follows a similar model with their Freddy AI at $29/agent/month on top of base seats.

This model is predictable per agent but adds up fast when you need multiple seats plus AI.

### Flat Monthly Pricing

**Ansa** charges a flat monthly fee regardless of how many tickets the AI resolves:

- Starter: $38/month or $348/year
- Growth: $129/month or $1,188/year
- Pro: $389/month or $3,588/year

No per-ticket charges. No per-seat multipliers. The AI resolves 500 tickets or 5,000 — same price.

A smaller Shopify store doing ~2,000 orders/month typically generates around 500 support tickets. Let's assume AI handles 75% of them (375 tickets) and humans handle the rest.

| Model | Monthly Cost | Cost Per AI Ticket |

At 500 tickets/month, every AI option saves money vs manual-only. The differences between providers are modest — a few hundred dollars. But watch what happens when volume increases.

The pattern is clear: per-ticket pricing punishes growth. The more effective your AI becomes, the more you pay. Flat pricing rewards it.

## Which Pricing Model Is Right for Your Store?

The answer depends on where you are today and where you're headed:

**Per-ticket pricing makes sense if:**
- You're testing AI for the first time and want low upfront commitment
- Your ticket volume is under 200/month
- You're not sure how many tickets AI will actually resolve

**Per-seat pricing makes sense if:**
- You're already locked into Zendesk or Freshdesk and adding AI incrementally
- You have a small, fixed support team and predictable volume

**Flat pricing makes sense if:**
- You're doing 500+ tickets/month (the savings are immediate)
- You want cost predictability — no surprise bills
- You plan to scale (more orders shouldn't mean proportionally more cost)
- You want the AI to get better without your bill getting bigger

For most Shopify stores past the early stage, flat pricing wins on pure economics. A store doing 1,000 tickets/month saves $1,000-$8,000/month switching from manual to AI support — and with flat pricing, those savings are locked in regardless of volume growth.
