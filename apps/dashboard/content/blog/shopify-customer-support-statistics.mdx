Customer support is the silent margin killer for Shopify stores. It scales linearly with order volume, eats into profit on every ticket, and one bad interaction can lose a customer forever.

But most store owners make support decisions based on gut feeling, not data. This post changes that. We've compiled 75+ statistics from Gartner, Forrester, Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, NRF, and other authoritative sources to give you the full picture of ecommerce customer support in 2026.

Whether you're deciding to invest in AI, hire another agent, or rethink your returns policy — these numbers will help you make the case.

## WISMO & Order Inquiry Statistics

"Where is my order?" is the single most common customer support question in ecommerce. These stats show just how much WISMO dominates support queues — and how much it costs.

1. **WISMO accounts for 30-40% of all ecommerce support tickets**, rising to 50%+ during peak shopping seasons like Black Friday and the holidays. ([Salesforce Commerce Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/commerce/wismo/))

2. **91% of consumers actively track their packages**, with 39% checking once per day and 19% checking multiple times per day. ([Verte/Propeller Insights, 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220420005141/en/Verte-Research-Reveals-Consumers-Are-Obsessed-With-Order-Tracking-Visibility))

3. **90% of consumers want the ability to track their delivery orders** — yet 1 in 3 were unable to do so. ([DispatchTrack, 2022](https://www.dispatchtrack.com/blog/consumers-track-deliver-orders/))

4. **80% of consumers want frequent delivery updates**; 66% want daily updates at minimum. ([DispatchTrack, 2022](https://www.dispatchtrack.com/blog/consumers-track-deliver-orders/))

5. **Each WISMO ticket costs $5-$22 when handled by a human agent**, consuming 3-5 minutes of agent time for lookup, interpretation, and response. ([Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/blog/automate-wismo-requests))

6. **70% of consumers prefer self-service tracking over contacting support** when a shipment is delayed. ([Verte/Propeller Insights, 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220420005141/en/Verte-Research-Reveals-Consumers-Are-Obsessed-With-Order-Tracking-Visibility))

7. **69% of shoppers will use self-service resources before contacting support.** ([eDesk](https://www.edesk.com/blog/ecommerce-customer-service-statistics/))

8. **Proactive shipping notifications reduce WISMO inquiries by 50-80%**, often within the first 30 days of implementation. ([ShippyPro, 2026](https://www.blog.shippypro.com/en/how-to-reduce-wismo-tickets-in-ecommerce-the-complete-guide))

9. **Proactive notifications deflect up to 60% of WISMO calls**, and branded tracking pages drive 3x more engagement per order. ([Narvar](https://corp.narvar.com/track))

10. **86% of service agents say customer expectations are higher than ever**, with 76% of service orgs anticipating higher case volumes ahead. ([Salesforce State of Service, 2024](https://www.salesforce.com/service/state-of-service-report/))

11. **Capezio achieved a 90% reduction in WISMO calls** through proactive communication — a real-world case study of what's possible. ([WISMOlabs](https://wismolabs.com/blog/effective-strategies-to-reduce-where-is-my-order-inquiries/))

<Callout variant="tip">
**Key Takeaway:** WISMO tickets are the lowest-value, highest-volume work in your support queue. They're repetitive, predictable, and cost the same as complex tickets when a human handles them. Proactive notifications and AI can eliminate 50-90% of them.
</Callout>

## Customer Support Cost Statistics

Most Shopify store owners underestimate their true support costs. These numbers show what you're actually paying — and where the money goes.

1. **Live agent support costs $8.01 per contact** compared to just $0.10 for self-service — an 80x difference. ([Gartner, 2019](https://www.gartner.com/smarterwithgartner/rethink-customer-service-strategy-drive-self-service))

2. **Average cost per ticket in retail/ecommerce ranges from $2.70 to $5.60.** ([MaestroQA, 2024](https://livechatai.com/blog/customer-support-cost-benchmarks))

3. **Tier 1 support resolution costs ~$22/ticket; Tier 3 escalation costs $104+/ticket.** ([MetricNet, 2024](https://www.metricnet.com/service-desk-cost-per-ticket-motm/))

4. **Contact center agent turnover rates run 30-45% annually**, creating a constant cycle of hiring and training. ([QATC/ICMI, via Plivo](https://www.plivo.com/blog/hidden-costs-of-customer-service-agent-turnover-and-how-to-reduce-them/))

5. **Replacing a single support agent costs $10,000-$20,000** when you factor in recruiting, training, and ramp-up time. ([SHRM/Gallup, via Plivo](https://www.plivo.com/blog/hidden-costs-of-customer-service-agent-turnover-and-how-to-reduce-them/))

6. **Only 14% of customer service issues are fully resolved through self-service channels** — highlighting the gap between self-service investment and actual deflection. ([Gartner, 2024](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-08-19-gartner-survey-finds-only-14-percent-of-customer-service-issues-are-fully-resolved-in-self-service))

7. **75% of customer service reps reported their highest-ever ticket volume in 2024.** ([HubSpot State of Customer Service, 2024](https://blog.hubspot.com/service/customer-service-stats))

8. **SaaS companies spend ~8% of annual recurring revenue on customer support and success.** ([SaaS Capital, 2025](https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/spending-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-saas-companies/))

9. **AI-enabled service agents saw a 14% increase in issue resolution per hour.** ([McKinsey, 2023](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai))

10. **Sellers on 3+ marketplaces without unified software average 7.5-hour response times** and 40% more support volume from duplicate inquiries. ([eDesk](https://www.edesk.com/blog/ecommerce-customer-service-statistics/))

11. **Combining self-service + AI chatbots reduces average support cost per interaction from $4.50 to $2.10** — a 53% reduction. ([Ringly.io, 2026](https://www.ringly.io/blog/ecommerce-customer-support-statistics-2026))

<Callout variant="tip">
**Key Takeaway:** The real cost of support isn't just agent wages — it's turnover, training, tools, and idle time. A single escalated ticket can cost $100+. The economics strongly favor automating simple, repetitive tickets and reserving human agents for high-value conversations.
</Callout>

## Ecommerce Returns & Refunds Statistics

Returns are the second-largest driver of support tickets after WISMO. For Shopify stores, return policy and process directly impact margins and repeat purchase rates.

1. **Total retail returns reached $890 billion in 2024.** ([NRF & Happy Returns, 2024](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/nrf-and-happy-returns-report-2024-retail-returns-total-890-billion))

2. **The overall online return rate is 16.9%**, with ecommerce-specific returns projected at 19.3% for 2025. ([NRF, 2025](https://nrf.com/research/2025-retail-returns-landscape))

3. **Processing a return costs retailers an average of 30% of the item's original price.** ([Optoro, via NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/retail-returns-890-billion-problem-rcna183359))

4. **Return fraud cost retailers $103 billion in 2024** — 15.14% of all returns. ([NRF & Happy Returns, 2024](https://nrf.com/research/2024-consumer-returns-retail-industry))

5. **82% of consumers cite free returns as a major factor in purchase decisions**, up from 76% the prior year. ([NRF, 2025](https://nrf.com/research/2025-retail-returns-landscape))

6. **71% of consumers are less likely to shop at a retailer after a poor returns experience**, up from 67% in 2024. ([NRF, 2025](https://nrf.com/research/2025-retail-returns-landscape))

7. **70% of Shopify merchants now charge return fees**, up from 65% in 2024. ([Loop Returns, 2025](https://www.loopreturns.com/report/2025-trend-report-strengthening-ecommerce-operations/))

8. **39% of consumers return items purchased online at least once per month.** ([Narvar, 2024](https://www.retaildive.com/news/online-shopper-returns-behavior/725206/))

9. **57% of shoppers admitted to engaging in fraudulent return behavior** at least once (dropping to 37% in 2025). ([Narvar, 2024](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/narvar-state-of-returns-2024-report-highlights-growing-impact-of-returns-on-retail-margins-and-customer-loyalty-302227590.html))

10. **Returns generated 8.4 billion pounds of landfill waste in 2023.** ([Optoro, 2023](https://www.optoro.com/returns-blog/optoro-impact-report-2023/))

<Callout variant="tip">
**Key Takeaway:** Returns are expensive (30% of item price) and frequent (nearly 1 in 5 online orders). The trend toward charging return fees is accelerating — 70% of Shopify merchants now do it. But 71% of customers will leave after a bad return experience, so the process matters as much as the policy.
</Callout>

11. **73% of customers rate live chat as their highest-satisfaction support channel.** ([ICMI Benchmark Study](https://www.helpscout.com/blog/live-chat-statistics/))

**Key Takeaway:** Live chat isn't just a support channel — it's a revenue channel. 2.8x higher conversion rates and 60% higher order values make the ROI case clear. The shift now is from human-staffed chat to AI-first chat with human escalation, where chatbots handle the routine and agents handle the complex.
