## Overview

Self-hosting Ansa on your own infrastructure is possible for enterprise customers who require full control over their data and deployment.

## Interested in Self-Hosting?

If you'd like to self-host Ansa, please [contact us](mailto:support@ansa.ai) for more information about:

- Deployment requirements and architecture
- Licensing and pricing
- Technical support and maintenance
- Custom integrations

Our team will work with you to understand your needs and provide the appropriate guidance for your self-hosted deployment.
